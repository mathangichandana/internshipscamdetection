💡 How InternGuard Works (Hybrid Methodology)
1. Rule Engine

Regex patterns detect high-risk signals: registration fees, unrealistic stipend, 'no interview', 'guaranteed job', urgent phrasing.

2. URL & Email Inspector

Inspects links for missing HTTPS, direct IP hosts, abnormal URL length, and flags personal webmail domains (Gmail, Yahoo).

3. Machine Learning

TF-IDF converts textual n-grams into feature vectors, and Logistic Regression computes a probabilistic scam score.

4. Risk Fusion

Combines rules (50%) and ML probability (50%) into a unified 0-100 score, categorized into Low, Moderate, High, or Very High.
